Edge is your price × the fair chance − 1, so +3% means you'd expect about $3 back for every $100 staked over many similar bets. The fair chance includes the bookmaker being checked, which keeps the edge on the cautious side. Ladbrokes and Neds count once. Player lines appear here once you've loaded them on Live odds; one-sided markets like anytime scorer are left out because their fair chance can't be worked out. Stakes use the Kelly formula scaled down to your setting and capped, because a single price can be wrong. Prices marked "may be stale" haven't updated as recently as the rest of the market, which is a common reason for a price looking generous.

A multi only pays when every leg wins, and each leg carries a bookmaker margin, so more legs means more margin against you. That's why singles near your target usually rank highest.
